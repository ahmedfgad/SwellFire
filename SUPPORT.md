# Swellfire support

For help with Swellfire, email
[ahmed.f.gad@gmail.com](mailto:ahmed.f.gad@gmail.com) or open a
[public support issue](https://github.com/ahmedfgad/SwellFire/issues).

When reporting a problem, include:

- the device model and operating-system version;
- the Swellfire version and build number;
- what you expected and what happened; and
- steps that reproduce the problem, if known.

Do not post Apple IDs, passwords, signing certificates, provisioning profiles,
IP addresses, or other private information in a public issue.

Multiplayer requires Local Network permission and both devices must be able to
reach the host's TCP port. Solo play does not require Local Network access.

Privacy information is available in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
