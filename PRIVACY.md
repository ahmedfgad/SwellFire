# Swellfire privacy policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Swellfire is a game by Ahmed Fawzy Gad. It has no user accounts, advertising,
analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. The developer does not receive or
store your saved progress, settings, gameplay, contacts, location, photos,
microphone data, advertising identifier, or other personal content.

## Data stored on your device

Game progress, stars, coins, unlocked items, and settings are stored locally on
your device. They are not uploaded to the developer. Uninstalling Swellfire
removes this local app data, subject to your device's normal backup behavior.

## Multiplayer and network use

Multiplayer is optional. If you host or join a two-player game, Swellfire sends
game state and control messages directly between the two devices at the IP
address and port entered by the players. The developer does not operate a game
server and does not receive or retain these messages.

When you open the Host screen, Swellfire may contact one of these public-address
services over HTTPS: `api.ipify.org`, `ifconfig.me`, or `icanhazip.com`. The sole
purpose is to display your internet-facing IP address so you can share it with
the other player. These services necessarily receive the connecting IP address
and may process operational logs under their own policies. Swellfire does not
send them gameplay, account, advertising, or device-identifier data; it does not
retain the returned address after the app session or use it for tracking.

iOS also asks for Local Network access when you use multiplayer. Denying that
permission prevents nearby-device multiplayer but does not prevent solo play.

## Security and sharing

Public-address lookups use certificate-verified HTTPS. Swellfire does not sell
personal information or share it for advertising, cross-app tracking, or data
brokerage. Direct multiplayer traffic is not routed through the developer.
